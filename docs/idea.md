I want to build a water safety monitoring system. a device that checks water quality based on tds and other metrics based on WHO and determines if the water is safe to drink or use. That sensor will send data to our application what will be monitored by operators who will monitor which location has what type of water situation. If anywhere water gets contaminated the sensor will detect that and with that information system will generate a risk assessment and warn the operators. Operator will then take action to resolve that situation by taking necessary action. This flow will ensure water is always safe to use by the consumer. This system can be used anywhere in to monitor water safety in cities where pipe leakage can cause contamination in water or by any accidents.
