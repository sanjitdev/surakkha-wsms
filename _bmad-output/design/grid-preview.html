5 · Empty States (8 patterns)

Each empty uses card--with-heading as container; icon at --icon-size-xl (24 px) --fg-tertiary; heading --font-size-md; body --font-size-sm. CTAs are optional (0, 1, or 2).

No sensors reporting

Add your first sensor to start collecting turbidity readings from this ward.

No incidents in this ward

No reports in the past 24 hours. The sensor cluster is calm.

No chain entries yet

The chain will appear here as the first sensor readings are anchored.

No Anjali reports

Share the reporting link with residents to gather ground-truth signals.

No results for that query

Try fewer or different keywords, or clear the search to see all entries.

No entries match this filter

Adjust the filter or clear it to see the full list.

All caught up

No pending reports. The ward is quiet — go stretch your legs.

Ward mismatch

You're scoped to ward 07. Switch accounts or contact your supervisor to access this ward.
